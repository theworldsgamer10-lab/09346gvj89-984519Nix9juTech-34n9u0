Nix Tech

Short, clear explanations of how things actually work.

Nix Tech is a personal YouTube channel that publishes one-minute educational Shorts: the mechanism behind an everyday phenomenon, explained in plain language, across physics, biology, history, engineering and the rest of the school curriculum.

This site exists for one practical reason: Google's OAuth consent screen requires a public home page, privacy policy and terms of service before a personal automation tool can be authorised to upload on the channel owner's behalf.

What this application does

  • Generates and renders short educational videos from a topic list.
  • Uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • Uses only the youtube.upload permission, for the operator's own account.
  • Collects no data about any other person, and has no other users.
